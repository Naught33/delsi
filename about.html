About Delsi Meat Solutions

Your trusted Partner in Butchery and Meat Processing Solutions.

Delsi Meat Solutions Ltd is a Nairobi-based supplier of butchery equipment, meat processing machines and spare parts. From our base at 53 Park Ring Road in Kilimani, we serve butchers, hotels, restaurants, supermarkets and meat processors across Kenya.

What we do

Equipment for every stage of the work

Our catalogue covers the equipment a meat business uses every day, from cutting and mincing to packing, chilling and slicing. We also supply the parts and consumables that keep machines running.

If you are setting up a new butchery, replacing a machine or looking for a specific spare, talk to us on WhatsApp and we will help you identify the right item.

  • Ice makers and cold roomsCold storage and ice equipment for meat businesses
  • Packaging and slicingVacuum packaging, paper, plastic and slicers
  • Spare partsBearings, motors, knives, plates and accessories
